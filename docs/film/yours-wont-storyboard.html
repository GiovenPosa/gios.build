Yours won't.

A 37-second film for gios.build that answers what a small business owner actually wants to know: what's wrong with my website, what would you do about it, how does it work, what do I get and what does it cost. It follows an example business from a dated site with no enquiries to a crafted new one that brings them in, told in the site's own language: Plus Jakarta, Fragment Mono section bars, a red dot as the one accent, and the site's fast-then-soft-stop motion.

How everything moves

01Value first

Every shot answers a question a customer has. Nothing is there just to look clever.

02Transform, don't cut

The old site's window carries into the next shot and rebuilds into the new design, so the change is the story.

03Fast, then a soft stop

The site's own curve on every move: quick off the mark, landing gently. No stepped growth, no flicker, no hard-cut bursts.

04Words from one side

Lines arrive word by word from the right, about 0.1s apart. Emphasis is a drawn red line, never bold or colour.

05Red, used once

The red dot marks the one thing that matters in a shot: the section bar, the step being reached, an enquiry arriving.

06Examples are labelled

The business, its enquiries and its booking are made up, and say so on screen. Swap in a real client when there is one.

Open questions

  1. A real client. Shots 02, 03 and 05 use a made-up plumber. A real before and after, with real enquiries, would make the film far more convincing.
  2. Where it lives. Home page, Packages page, Instagram, or all three. A social cut wants 15 to 20 seconds: shots 01, 03, 06 and 07.
  3. Sound. It works muted. The score is a warm marimba and soft electric piano; tell me if it should be quieter, or go.
  4. Your face. The end card could carry your portrait beside the wordmark, to make it personal.

This is version 2. Version 1 borrowed motion techniques studied in OpenAI's Refreshed. film (stepped states, bursts of hard cuts) and was dropped: it said nothing a customer needed, and the stepped growth looked glitchy on video. Nothing from that film is used here.
